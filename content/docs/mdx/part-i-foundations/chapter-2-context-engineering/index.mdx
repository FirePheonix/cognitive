# Chapter 2 — Context Engineering

If Chapter 1 introduced the architecture of AI systems, Chapter 2 goes inside the most important layer of that architecture — the context. Context engineering is the discipline of deciding what information the model should receive, how to assemble it, how to manage it over time, and how to keep it relevant as applications scale.

This chapter covers the full spectrum of context engineering practice: from the philosophical distinction between prompting and context engineering, through the infrastructure of harness engineering, to the operational concerns of prompt versioning and agent workflow design.
