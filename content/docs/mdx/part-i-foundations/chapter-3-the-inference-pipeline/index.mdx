# Chapter 3 — The Inference Pipeline

When you send a prompt to a language model, a precise sequence of operations unfolds before you see a single word of response. This chapter traces that sequence end-to-end — from the moment text is converted into tokens, through the parallel computation of prefill and the sequential rhythm of decode, to the streaming protocol that delivers tokens to your screen in real time.

Understanding the inference pipeline isn't just academic. It tells you where latency comes from, how to optimize it, what can go wrong and why, and how the engineering choices you make in the context layer connect to the computational reality of what happens inside the model.
