# Appendix D — Building GPT

This appendix is about building GPT from scratch — not just reading about it, but actually constructing every component, understanding why each piece exists, and watching a model learn to generate text.

![Appendix D in five cards: GPT architecture, the next-token objective, the training loop, generation, and a from-scratch notebook.](/appendix-d/building-gpt-arc.png)

Appendix C covered the transformer block in detail: self-attention, multi-head attention, positional encoding, and the feed-forward network. This appendix assembles those pieces into a complete language model, trains it, and generates text.

By the end, you'll understand:
- Why GPT is called "decoder-only" and what that means structurally
- How next-token prediction works as a training objective, with real numbers
- What the training loop looks like step-by-step, including learning rate scheduling
- How autoregressive generation works, and what temperature, top-k, and top-p actually do
- How to build the entire thing from scratch in one complete notebook

## Chapters

**Chapter 11 — GPT Architecture**
The decoder-only architecture from top to bottom. What makes GPT different from an encoder, how the causal mask works, and a parameter breakdown for real models from GPT-2 small to GPT-3.

**Chapter 12 — Training GPT: Data and the Objective**
What training data looks like as token sequences, how input/target pairs are created by shifting by one position, and how cross-entropy loss measures the model's surprise at seeing the next token. Includes real token numbers.

**Chapter 13 — The Training Loop**
The five steps that run on every batch: forward pass, compute loss, zero gradients, backward pass, optimizer step. Plus learning rate warmup and cosine decay explained with diagrams, and gradient clipping. A complete training loop that runs a tiny GPT on Shakespeare data and shows loss going down.

**Chapter 14 — Inference and Text Generation**
Autoregressive generation step by step. Greedy decoding, temperature sampling, top-k, and top-p explained with real examples. A complete `generate()` function with all sampling modes and side-by-side output comparisons at different temperatures.

**Chapter 15 — Building GPT From Scratch: The Complete Notebook**
The crown jewel. One complete notebook that builds everything — tokenizer, embeddings, self-attention, multi-head attention, transformer block, the full GPT model, training loop, and generation — from absolute zero. Every step is explained. The loss goes down. Text gets generated. This is the whole picture in one place.
