# Long Form

Long-form deep dives into every layer of production AI systems. These are the main chapters of the AI Systems handbook — each one covers a topic end-to-end with the full engineering detail.

---

## Part I — Foundations

The mental model of how modern AI systems work. The lifecycle of an AI request, context engineering, prompt construction, and the inference pipeline from tokenization to streaming output.

## Part II — High Performance Inference

KV cache internals, continuous batching, flash attention, speculative decoding, and model compression. How to make LLMs fast and cheap at scale.

## Part III — Building AI Applications

Structured outputs, function calling, agent engineering, model routing, and multimodal agents. The engineering patterns for building reliable AI-powered products.

## Part IV — Retrieval Systems

RAG from scratch, advanced retrieval, reranking, and retrieval evaluation. How to give models access to knowledge they were not trained on.

## Part V — Evaluation

Golden datasets, benchmark design, LLM-as-a-judge, continuous evaluation, and A/B testing. How to know whether your system is actually working.

## Part VI — Production Engineering

Observability, cost engineering, reliability engineering, and loop engineering. The discipline of running AI systems in production.

## Part VII — AI Security

Prompt injection, jailbreaks, data leakage, permission boundaries, and multi-tenant isolation. The threat model for AI systems.

## Part VIII — Choosing the Right Technique

When to use prompt engineering vs RAG vs fine-tuning vs distillation. Decision frameworks and engineering tradeoffs.

## Part IX — Failure Engineering

The failure modes that kill production AI systems, and how to diagnose and prevent them.

## Part X — Field Notes

Shorter essays on tools that showed up in public. Reverse Engineer Anything is the first: what the connector actually does, and what a star count does not.
