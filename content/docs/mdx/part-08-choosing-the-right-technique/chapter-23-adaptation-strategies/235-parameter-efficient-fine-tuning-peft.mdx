# 23.5 Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT)

This section covers the details of 23.5 Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT).

PEFT trains a small add-on and leaves the base model frozen. LoRA (Hu et al., 2021) is the one that stuck. A weight matrix W stays as it is. The update is a product of two thin matrices, B and A, with rank r much smaller than the hidden size. For a 4096×4096 matrix and r = 8 you train 2 × 4096 × 8 = 65,536 numbers instead of about 16 million, and at inference you can fold BA back into W so there is no extra matmul.

![A frozen weight W plus a low-rank product B times A](/part-08/chapter-23/lora-ba.png)
*Fig: Hu et al. draw the same picture: x goes through W and through BA, and the outputs add. Only A and B get gradients.*

QLoRA (Dettmers et al., 2023) quantizes the frozen W to 4-bit so the base fits on one GPU, and still trains the LoRA in 16-bit. That is how a 65B-class adapter became a single-GPU experiment. The adapter still does not store your wiki. It stores a direction in weight space: style, format, a preference. Swap adapters per tenant if you must; do not bake tenant documents into one.
